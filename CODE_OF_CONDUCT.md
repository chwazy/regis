# Code of Conduct

The Régis project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for every issue, discussion and pull request.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good faith, give and receive constructive criticism
gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, personal or political
attacks, and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer,
[@chwazy](https://github.com/chwazy), through the contact shown on their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers apply the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (correction, warning,
temporary ban, permanent ban), depending on the impact of the behaviour.
